Race report

Why were deliveries arriving late?

A case from my current role. Operations knew on-time delivery was slipping but not where or why.

  1. Ask the right question

    Before touching data, I worked with Operations and Supply leadership to agree on what "on time and in full" meant and which delays mattered most to them.

  2. Clean 200,000 records

    Procurement, dispatch and delivery data lived in different shapes. I used Python and pandas to clean and trim it into something that could be trusted.

  3. Follow the failures

    In SQL, I traced each late or short delivery back through the chain to find the patterns behind it, rather than stopping at the first number that looked right.

  4. Put it on the pit wall

    I built Metabase dashboards so leadership could see delivery performance every day, replacing a manual spreadsheet.

  5. Twelve percent faster to the line

    The fixes leadership acted on contributed to a 12% improvement in on-time-in-full delivery within two months.
