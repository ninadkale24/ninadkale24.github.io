Put your files in this folder using these exact names:

  me.jpg                       your photo for the opening (portrait shape, at least 800px tall)
  Ninad_Kale_Resume.pdf        your resume

  dashboards/otif.png          Delivery performance (OTIF)
  dashboards/sellers.png       Seller performance
  dashboards/inventory.png     Inventory availability
  dashboards/finance.png       Finance reconciliation
  (blur any confidential numbers or names first)

Then open index.html, scroll to the very bottom, and fill in SETTINGS:
  instagramHandle    your handle without the @
  instagramPosts     links to 3 or 6 of your posts (on Instagram: open a post, tap ... then Copy link)
  CSPO_CERT_URL      your Scrum Alliance certificate link
  DATACAMP_CERT_URL  your DataCamp certificate link
